KINGDOM HEARTS QUAKE 4 MOD
This mod add Kingdom Hearts inspired elements to Quake 4, these include things such as custom Hud elements, magic, movement abilities, stats, and equippable items.
Main Features
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

New Stat system
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When defeating enemies, they will grant the player experience points. Gaining a certain amount of exp will cause the player to level up.
- Leveling up will increase these stats:
- Attack - Affects the blasters damage
- Defense - Reduces the amount of damage the player takes
- Speed - Affects how fast the player moves
- Magic - Changes the effectiveness, damage, and duration of the players magic
- Max Mana - The max amount of mana the player can have
- Max Health - The max amount of health the player has
- Max AP - The max amount of Action Points the player has

Whenever the player levels up, a notification will appear on screen
To views the stats press "\\" or backslash in game.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Magic System
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

My mod adds 6 spells to Quake 4. All of these spells use mana which is tracked on by the blue bar on the players screen
Mana is mainly gained by defeating enemies and leveling up.

These spells are:
- Fire - A high damage, high speed spell that does splash damage, damage increases with magic stat
- Blizzard - A mid damage, slow spell that freezes the enemy on contact for a duration relative to the magic stat, damage also increases with magic stat
- Thunder - A spell that lobs a group of low damage projectiles, damage increases with magic stat
- Aero - While active, this spell will halve the damage the player takes for a duration relative to the magic stat
- Cure - Heals the player, the amount healed increases with the magic stat
- Gravity - A mid damage, slow spell that launches the enemy on contact, the height increases with the magic stat

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Movement Abilities
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Some of these abilities will require the use of AP or Action Points, these are tracked by the orange bar on your screen and the amount increases as you level up. You can also gain AP by defeating enemies and leveling up
This mod add fives movement abilities
These abilities are:
- Double Jump - After jumping once, pressing space again will allow to jump a second time
- High Jump - Pressing V will allow you to jump really high, this drains AP
- Dash - Pressing Left shift while on the ground or in the air will allow you to dash, this drains AP
- Glide - While in the air, if you hold Tab, you can glide, this will drain AP overtime
- Ground Slam - While in the air, if you press Right Shift, you will pull yourself towards the ground and nullify any fall damage

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Equipment
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This mod adds 10 equippable items that can affect your stats, magic, and movement abilities
You can get these items by defeating enemies, an "Obtained New Item" message will appear when you do.
To see these items, go into the console with ctrl-alt-~ and type "ShowInventory"
This will allow you to see what Items you own and what you have equipped. You have a total of 3 equipment slots that you can fill 
To equip an item, type "equipItem" into the console followed by the name of the item as shown in your inventory
To unequip an item, type "unequipItem" into the console, followed by its name to remove its abilities
These are the 10 items added:

- RingofBlindingSpeed
  - Gives the player +20 in there speed stat, making them really fast
- RingofStrength
  - Increases the players strength by 5 but reduces the players magic by 5, requires that the player has 5 or more magic before equipping
- HeavyArmor
  - Increases the players defense by 6 but lowers their speed by 0.5, requires a speed of 0.5 or more to use
- FireRing
  - While equipped, the player casts double the fire with spread
- BerzerkerRing
  - While equipped, the players mana, magic, and defense are all set to 0 but their strength increases by 50
- BlizzardFireRing
  - While equipped, the players blizzard spells also casts fire in the same direction
- GlideRing
  - While equipped, the amount of AP the glides uses per frame is lowered, allowing the player to glide much faster
- CureRing
  - While equipped, whenever the player takes damage, they heal 1 third of the damage taken
- ManaRing
  - While equipped, the players mana replenishes over time
- ApRing
   - While equipped, the players Action Points replenish over time

**Controls**
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Magic controls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Fire - Q
- Blizzard - E
- Thunder - X
- Cure - C
- Aero - Z
- Gravity - F

Movement Abilities
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

V - High Jump
- Tab(While in Air) - Glide
- Left Shift - Dash
- Right Shift(While in Air) - Ground Slam
- Space(While in Air) - Double Jump

Other Controls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- H - Help Screen
- \ - Stats Screen
- In Console:
- ShowInventory - show your inventory
- EquipItem - equip an item 
- UnequipItem - unequips and item
